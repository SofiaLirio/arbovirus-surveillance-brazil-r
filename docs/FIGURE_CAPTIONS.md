# Figure captions (draft)

Draft captions for the figures in `figures/`, written to be pasted into the manuscript. Every
number comes from the generated caption material (run of 2026-10-13, SINAN release of
2026-10-06): `outputs/figure_caption_values.csv`, `outputs/figure_exclusions.csv` and
`outputs/figure_exclusions_captions.txt`. If the pipeline is rerun on newer data, update the
numbers from those files. The numbering of supplementary figures (S1, S2) is a placeholder.

Abbreviations used throughout: UF, federative unit (state); IRR, incidence rate ratio; CI,
confidence interval; FE, fixed effects; LISA, local indicator of spatial association.

## Main figures

**Figure 1. Annual and monthly incidence of dengue, chikungunya and Zika in Brazil, 2016–2026.**

**Figure 2. Temporal trends in annual incidence by state, 2016–2026.**

**Figure 4. Spatial autocorrelation of mean annual incidence.**

## Methods and limitations (text for the manuscript)

**Missing values.** In the clinical figures (Figures 5, 7 and 8), percentages and distributions
were calculated on records with a known value. Records coded as ignored or blank in SINAN
(`Ign/Blank`; for sex, `Ign` and `Blank`; for the state of notification, `Ign/exterior`) were
excluded from numerators and denominators. Recorded answers such as "not performed", "not
applicable" or "inconclusive" were kept as categories. The proportion excluded ranged from
<0.1% (age, sex) to 97.9% (dengue serotype) of notifications; the number and percentage excluded
for each figure and year are given in `outputs/figure_exclusions.csv`.

**Limitations of the data.**
- Dengue serotype was recorded for only 0.2–3.0% of notifications per year (547–189,618 typed cases),
  so Figure 7D describes typed cases only, which may not represent all cases.
- In 2025, 98% of dengue ELISA and IgM records were ignored/blank, compared with 35–53% (ELISA)
  and 34–49% (IgM) in 2016–2024, which suggests a change in how test results were recorded. The 2025 proportions
  in Figures 7A–B rest on about 34,000 records.
- The national SINAN files contain no chikungunya or Zika records for Espírito Santo in 2021,
  although the state reported 2,901 chikungunya and 872 Zika cases for January–October 2021
  through its own notification system (e-SUS VS)
  ([Governo do Espírito Santo, 2021](https://www.es.gov.br/Noticia/espirito-santo-registra-mais-de-2-mil-casos-de-chikungunya-em-2021)).
  That state-year is therefore missing, not zero. It is shown without a symbol in Supplementary
  Figure S1 and is absent from the annual series, so the mean incidence and trend of these two
  diseases in Espírito Santo use the other ten years.
- The clinical and demographic figures (5, 7, 8) use the GraphPad tables, which end in 2025 (and
  start in 2017 for chikungunya); all other analyses run to September 2026.
- 2026 covers January–September (SINAN release of 2026-10-06); annual values for 2026 are
  annualised from the reported months.
- In Supplementary Figure S1, classes are pooled over all years so that colours are comparable
  across years; years with low incidence therefore fall mostly in class 1.
